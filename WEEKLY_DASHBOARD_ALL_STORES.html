VII. Methodology Notes
Period: W35, Mon 2026-08-24 through Sun 2026-08-30, all 6 stores, 7/7 days each. Generated 2026-08-31.
Net sales: Aggregated from each store's own self-pulled Dutchie transaction export (Retail type, non-void, non-return, deduplicated by transactionId; net sales = totalBeforeTax). Reconciles exactly to each of the 6 already-approved store dashboards — no figures re-derived independently at the rollup level.
Labor: Worked Hours and Payroll Cost sourced from UKG Counters (DetailedCalculatedTimeCounters), standardized across all 6 stores effective W35. Rollup Blended $/hr = Payroll Cost ÷ Worked Hrs (payroll only, no security). Rollup Labor % of Net and Section I Network Labor % are security-inclusive: (Payroll Cost + Security Cost) ÷ Net Sales — deliberately different from each store dashboard's own Section I Labor % KPI, which is payroll-only. Sales:Labor $ = Net Sales ÷ (Payroll + Security).
WoW baseline: Each store's true prior week (W34, Mon 2026-08-17 – Sun 2026-08-23) self-pulled Dutchie export — never estimated or carried forward.
Store-level detail: Full per-store detail (roster, category performance, competitive landscape, YoY, data gaps) lives in each store's own 14-section weekly dashboard. This rollup intentionally carries no YoY content — see each store's own dashboard for its YoY comparison and any logged [GAP].
