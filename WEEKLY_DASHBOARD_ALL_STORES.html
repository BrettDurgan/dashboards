VII. Methodology Notes
Period: Mon 2026-08-31 – Sun 2026-09-06 (W36), all 6 stores. Generated 2026-09-08.
Net sales: Each store's Net Sales = Sec 2.1 filter (transactionType==Retail, non-void, non-return, deduped by transactionId, summed totalBeforeTax) from that store's own self-pulled Dutchie export; HLC Greenfield additionally date-window-filtered to its export's exact labeled 7-day range this cycle (see its own worksheet).
Labor: Labor % of Net = (Payroll Cost + Security Cost) ÷ Net Sales, including security — not payroll-only. Payroll Cost sourced from each store's UKG DetailedCalculatedTimeCounters ("Counters") export, never DetailedHours. Daily labor-cost figures in Section IV are an estimate (worked hours × store blended rate per day), since exact day-level payroll allocation is not separately tracked network-wide; all other labor figures are exact.
WoW baseline: Each store's own approved W35 build-worksheet figures (recomputed fresh from that store's raw W35 export where its own worksheet documents a correction, e.g. HLC Greenfield).
Store-level detail: Full 14-section detail (competitive landscape, category/SKU breakdowns, staffing heatmaps, YoY where available, data gaps) lives in each store's own dashboard file, delivered alongside this rollup.
