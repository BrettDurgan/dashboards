FLAG Timezone discovery this cycle (Lake Elsinore, La Mesa) — both California stores' raw Dutchie transactionDate field is stored in Eastern time company-wide rather than each store's real local time; day-bucketing and Saturday-gap validation now use transactionDateLocalTime at both stores to prevent late-evening transactions from being misassigned to the wrong calendar day. Massachusetts stores are unaffected (Eastern is their real local time).

VII. Methodology Notes
Period: Mon 2026-08-03 → Sun 2026-08-09 (W32). Generated 2026-08-10.
