XIV. Methodology Notes
Period: Mon 2026-08-03 → Sun 2026-08-09. Generated 2026-08-10.
Net sales: Dutchie transaction_line_items GAP_BACKFILL (union of GAP_BACKFILL_2026-07-26_to_2026-08-08 and GAP_BACKFILL_2026-08-09_to_2026-08-10, deduped by transactionId), totalBeforeTax, Retail type, isVoid=false, isReturn=false, for Mon–Fri/Sun. Timezone note: this store's raw transactionDate field is in Eastern time (the system default) rather than Pacific; all day-bucketing and hour-of-day analysis uses transactionDateLocalTime instead, which corrects the ~3-hour offset and prevents late-evening transactions from being misassigned to the wrong calendar day. Sat 2026-08-08 uses a separately-sourced Dutchie Sales Report to close the truncated window; validated by matching both the pre-gap (before 8:17am) and post-gap (after 5:07pm) boundary figures exactly against GAP_BACKFILL before trusting the full day.
SKU/category net-sales scaling: item totalPrice−totalDiscount (isReturned items skipped) summed per transaction, scaled by (transaction totalBeforeTax / summed item net) so item-level revenue reconciles to real net sales; applied before Section IX and the SKU tables. Residual vs. true totals: exact match (0.00%).
UKG hours: DetailedCalculatedTimeCounters 08/03–08/09 (master all-stores export, filtered to Department Full Path = "MOD/Lake Elsinore/Retail"; the separate "MOD/Lake Elsinore/Production" department excluded as non-customer-facing). Regular + Daily/FLSA Overtime. PTO/VAC/PSL/DFML/Excused excluded.
Staffing heatmap: UKG DetailedHours (master all-stores export, punch-level Start/End, filtered to Lake Elsinore Retail), allocated into 4 day-part windows (Morning 7a–11a, Midday 11a–3p, Afternoon 3p–6p, Evening 6p–9p per this store's own hours), overlapped against transactionDateLocalTime sales in the same windows.
