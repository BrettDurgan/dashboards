SKU/category net-sales scaling: item totalPrice−totalDiscount (isReturned items skipped) summed per transaction, scaled by (transaction totalBeforeTax / summed item net) so item-level revenue reconciles to real net sales; applied before Section IX and the SKU tables. Residual vs. true totals: 0.03%.
UKG hours: DetailedCalculatedTimeCounters 08/03–08/09 (master all-stores export, filtered to Department Full Path containing "NoHo"), EIN=Apical. Regular + Weekly Overtime. PTO/VAC/PSL/DFML/Excused excluded.
Staffing heatmap: UKG DetailedHours (master all-stores export, punch-level Start/End, filtered to NoHo), allocated into 4 day-part windows (Morning 8a–11a, Midday 11a–3p, Afternoon 3p–6p, Evening 6p–10p/close), overlapped against Mon–Fri/Sun transactionDateLocalTime sales and Sat Sales Report Order Time sales in the same windows.
