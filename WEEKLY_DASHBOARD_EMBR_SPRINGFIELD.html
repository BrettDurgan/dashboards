XIV. Methodology Notes
Period: Mon 2026-08-03 → Sun 2026-08-09. Generated 2026-08-10.
Net sales: Dutchie transaction_line_items, unioned across GAP_BACKFILL (7/26→8/3, 7/26→8/8, 8/9→8/10) and daily-incremental exports (8/4, 8/5, 8/6), deduped by transactionId (preferring the version with populated items), Retail type only, isVoid=false, isReturn=false. Item-level net = totalPrice−totalDiscount for non-returned items, scaled per-transaction by totalBeforeTax / sum(item_net) per standing methodology. Mon/Tue/Wed/Thu/Fri/Sun (6 of 7 days) are full, real transaction_line_items data. Sat 8/8 uses a separately-sourced Dutchie Sales Report (Order Time/Budtender Name/Net Sales/Category-level export covering the full calendar day) to close the 11:00am–7:59pm window that no transaction_line_items export covered; its before-11am Net Sales and Order ID count were cross-validated against the transaction_line_items figures for that window and matched exactly before being trusted.
